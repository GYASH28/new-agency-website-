Every project deserves a clear conversation. These are the basics we make sure you understand before committing.

Are these prices final?

No. They are published starting points. We confirm the complete scope, features, timeline and final amount in a written proposal before work begins.

A scoped website build delivered through a monthly engagement. The number of pages, revisions, engagement period, and launch arrangements are agreed before starting.

Are domain, hosting and paid tools included?

Not automatically. Hosting, domains, third-party subscriptions and paid AI APIs are quoted separately unless the project proposal explicitly includes them.
